Help & Guide

For Students

  • Go to the Library to upload your jobsheets.
  • Track your progress and level on the Workspace dashboard.
  • Once an instructor reviews your work, you'll see your mark and comment on the jobsheet card.

For Instructors

  • Sign in via the Instructor Portal to unlock review mode.
  • Open any jobsheet on the Workspace dashboard to mark and comment.
  • Marks can be updated any time — students see changes instantly.

Quick FAQ

Where's my data saved? Locally in this browser — no account needed for students.

Still stuck? Ask your instructor directly — this is a class project tool, not a helpdesk.

Privacy Policy

What We Store

  • All jobsheet data, progress, and settings stay in your browser's localStorage — nothing is uploaded to a server.
  • Uploaded files are stored locally too, tied to this browser only.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no third-party tracking.

Clearing Your Data

Clearing your browser storage for this site removes everything — there's no backup elsewhere, since this is a class project tool, not a production service.

Code of the Brick

  1. Every jobsheet you upload should be your own build — no borrowed bricks.
  2. Instructors grade in good faith; disputes go through your instructor directly, not this page.
  3. This tool runs entirely in your browser — use it, break it, learn from it. It's a class project, not a legal contract.
  4. Uninstalling/clearing the browser voids all progress. No refunds — there was nothing to pay for.
